📌 Project Overview
Supply chain reliability directly dictates operational expenditure and customer satisfaction. This dashboard provides logistics managers and operations executives with actionable insights into carrier performance, commodity valuation, vendor distributions, and delivery punctuality.

🎯 Key Objectives:
Monitor Freight Economics: Analyze overall freight costs and cost-per-kilogram across transport modes.
Track Punctuality: Measure Month-over-Month On-Time Delivery (OTD) rates.
Evaluate Transport Modes: Contrast delivery delay averages across Air, Truck, and Ocean shipments.
Identify Key Vendors & Brands: Map shipment volumes across primary suppliers and product brands.

💡 Key Business Insights
Truck & Air Charter Dominate Delays: Shipments under dedicated charter or road transport exhibit the highest average schedule delays compared to standard ocean lanes.
Air Freight Volume Dominance: Over 59% of order distributions utilize air logistics, driving up the average cost-per-kilogram.
Delivery Rate Volatility: Punctuality has trended downward over extended quarters, signaling a need for route re-allocation and tighter SLA enforcement with line-haul vendors.
